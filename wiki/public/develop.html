Develop

Requires JDK 25. 1.20.1 compiles with --release 17, and 1.21.x with 21.

./gradlew clean test buildAll

The jars are in build/libs/.

Targets

To build one target:

./gradlew -PonlyTarget=1.21.7 :1.21.7:build

See multi-loader development for native builds, pinned dependencies and real-client tests.
