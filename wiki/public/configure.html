Configure

Open Mod Menu → Cinematic Zoom Extended → Settings, or your loader's mod list on Forge and NeoForge.

Settings

Saved in config/cinematiczoom/config.json. Delete it to reset.

Setting Default Notes
Starting zoom 3× Per profile.
Black bars height 15% Cinematic profile, with the Legacy aspect ratio. ↑ ↓ adjust it by 1%.
Zoom in and out duration 240 ms Per profile.
Zoom in and out curve Exponential Per profile.
Hide HUD, crosshair and hands while zooming On Per profile.
Toggle zoom Off Hold the key by default.
Tick when scrolling On A soft tick for each wheel step, higher going in and lower going out.
Zoom sounds On The spyglass sounds when a zoom starts and ends.
Show zoom percentage On Shows the magnification above the hotbar when a zoom starts or you scroll.
Screenshot shutter On Black bars snap shut and open with a click on each screenshot.
Full frame rate HUD while zooming On Switches off HUD frame-rate limiters such as Gnetum while a zoom is on screen, so the bars stay smooth.
Spyglass zoom Off Cinematic or Regular makes a spyglass zoom with that profile instead of its scope.
Mouse wheel during zoom On Changes magnification while zooming.
Allow ultrawide zoom Off Allows zooming out to 0.5×.
Target values Empty Per profile. See Snap targets.

Older configs, including Cinematic Zoom 1.3's, migrate on launch, with a backup in config/cinematiczoom/backups. With no config yet, the first launch copies settings from Zoomify, Ok Zoomer, Just Zoom or any JSON or TOML file with zoom in its path, and leaves those files as they are.
