Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
requires cloth-config2 or modmenu, or no settings screenInstall Cloth Config and Mod Menu for your version.
incompatible with cinematiczoomKeep one Cinematic Zoom jar in mods.
Crash on launchCheck Java: 17, 21 or 25, per the install table.
Zoom key does nothingBind Cinematic Zoom under Controls.
Wheel does not zoomTurn on Mouse wheel during zoom, and hold the zoom key.
No barsUse the cinematic key (C) with a bar height above 0.
Broken configDelete config/cinematiczoom.json.

Report issues with logs/latest.log and your Minecraft, mod and loader versions.

Limits

  • Other zoom mods fight over the FOV. Run one; Extended imports their settings instead.
  • Quilt works through its Fabric support. OptiFine is unsupported.
  • Sodium, Iris and shader packs: no known conflicts, though not specifically tested.
