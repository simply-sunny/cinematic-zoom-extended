Cinematic Zoom Extended

A configurable zoom with animated cinema bars and two profiles.

Hold a key and the view eases in while bars slide across the screen. Let go and it eases back. Runs on Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge, from 1.20.1 to 26.3.
