Cinematic Zoom Extended

Smooth, configurable zoom with two profiles and animated cinema bars.

The zoom view needs JavaScript.

Hold a key and the view eases in while black bars slide across the screen. Release the key and it eases back out. Two independent profiles, six easing curves, ultra-wide zoom, and snap targets like 2x or 45fov. It also scales mouse sensitivity, removes view bobbing, and hides the HUD, crosshair, and hands independently. Runs on Fabric, Quilt, Forge, and NeoForge, from 1.20.1 to 26.3.
