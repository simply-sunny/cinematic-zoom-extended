Use

The view below runs the mod's zoom code over the title-screen panorama. Click it, then use the keys; Esc lets go of the mouse. On touch, drag.

The zoom view needs JavaScript.

KeyDoes
CCinematic zoom, with bars. Hold, or press once with Toggle zoom on.
VZoom without bars, with its own settings.
WheelChange magnification while zoomed.
↑ ↓Bar height, 1% a press (Legacy framing).
← →Cycle aspect ratios: Auto, 2.39, 2.35, 2, 1.85, 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, 1:1, Legacy. Wider than the window adds top and bottom bars, narrower adds side bars.

Rebind under Controls → Key Binds → Cinematic Zoom. Nothing zooms while a screen such as the inventory is open.
