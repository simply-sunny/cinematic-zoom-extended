Use

Try it below: click the view, then hold C (or V for no bars), scroll, press ↑ and ↓, and move the mouse to look around. The click locks the mouse to the view; Esc lets go. On a touch screen, drag instead. It's the mod's own zoom code, ported, over the game's title screen panorama.

The zoom view needs JavaScript.

Action Default key
Cinematic Zoom (hold or toggle) C
Zoom without bars V
Increase bar size ↑
Decrease bar size ↓

All keys are rebindable under Options → Controls → Key Binds → Cinematic Zoom.

Toggle zoom makes the key press once for on and once for off. Zoom and the bars stop while a screen such as the inventory is open.
